Get started

  1. Drag LOCKqz Sync (below) to your bookmarks bar.
  2. Open Brightspace and click it. Tick the quizzes LOCKqz should protect.
  3. Tell students to install LOCKqz once from lockdownqz.pages.dev.
  4. During the quiz, click Watch to see every student live.
